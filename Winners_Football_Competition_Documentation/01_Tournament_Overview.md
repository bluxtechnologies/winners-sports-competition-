# 01 --- Tournament Overview and Concept

## 1. Competition identity

**Name:** Winners Football Competition\
**Short name:** WFC\
**Host institution:** Southern Delta University, Ozoro\
**Edition:** First Edition\
**Location:** Southern Delta University, Ozoro\
**Dates and venue:** \[TO CONFIRM\]

## 2. Concept

Winners Football Competition is a student-focused, 5-a-side Monkey Post
football tournament organized for current students of Southern Delta
University, Ozoro. The first edition will use a 16-team
single-elimination knockout format, with team registration, published
fixtures and results, match officials, phone-based video
recording/review, and volunteer social media coverage.

## 3. Objectives

-   Provide an organized and enjoyable football competition for
    students.
-   Promote sportsmanship, fair play and campus participation.
-   Operate a transparent registration, draw, disciplinary and prize
    process.
-   Create matchday content and highlights for Instagram and TikTok.
-   Establish a repeatable foundation for future editions.

## 4. Competition snapshot

  Item                     Approved structure
  ------------------------ ---------------------------------------------
  Eligible participants    Current SDU Ozoro students only
  Number of teams          16
  Squad size               7 players per team
  Players on court/pitch   5 per team
  Playing format           Monkey Post, small goalposts
  Fixed goalkeeper         None
  Fixed post defender      None; all five players move freely
  Substitutes              2 per team
  Substitution limit       Maximum 2 substitutions per match
  Substitution type        Permanent; substituted player cannot return
  Match duration           20 minutes per half
  Knockout tie             5 minutes extra time, then penalties
  Registration fee         ₦10,000 per team
  Prize commitments        ₦50,000 / ₦20,000 / ₦10,000
  Tournament length        Minimum 2 weeks
  Media                    Volunteer friends; Instagram and TikTok
  Physical trophy/medals   Not provided in first edition

## 5. Competition format

The 16 accepted teams will be placed into a random draw. The knockout
tree contains: - Round of 16: 8 matches. - Quarter-finals: 4 matches. -
Semi-finals: 2 matches. - Third-place playoff: 1 match between the
losing semi-finalists. - Final: 1 match.

**Total scheduled matches: 16**, including the third-place playoff.

## 6. Prize distribution

  Finish                Prize
  ------------- -------------
  Champion            ₦50,000
  Runner-up           ₦20,000
  Third place         ₦10,000
  **Total**       **₦80,000**

No cup or medals will be provided in the first edition.

## 7. Media

Volunteer friends will support photography, video recording, match
highlights, pre-match posts, post-match updates and interviews. Official
channels: Instagram and TikTok. Volunteer support does not remove the
need to agree responsibilities, match coverage and consent procedures.

## 8. Success measures

-   16 eligible teams registered and paid.
-   All fixtures completed or formally resolved.
-   Results and disciplinary decisions recorded consistently.
-   Prize commitments paid as announced.
-   Participants receive clear communication.
-   No avoidable serious safety or integrity incidents.
-   Media content published with appropriate participant consent.
