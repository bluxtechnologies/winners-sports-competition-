# 08 --- Fixtures, Results and Knockout Management

## 1. Format

WFC uses a 16-team single-elimination knockout format. A team that loses
a knockout match is eliminated from the championship bracket, except
that the two losing semi-finalists play a separate third-place match.

## 2. Match count

  Stage                   Teams   Matches
  --------------------- ------- ---------
  Round of 16                16         8
  Quarter-finals              8         4
  Semi-finals                 4         2
  Third-place playoff         2         1
  Final                       2         1
  **Total**                 ---    **16**

## 3. Random draw

1.  Only 16 confirmed teams enter the draw.
2.  The draw is random.
3.  Each team is assigned a bracket position.
4.  The draw should be conducted transparently, preferably with
    witnesses.
5.  The bracket and resulting fixtures should be published after
    verification.
6.  Once published, bracket changes require a documented administrative
    reason and notice to affected teams.

## 4. Knockout progression

-   Round of 16 winners advance to the quarter-finals.
-   Quarter-final winners advance to the semi-finals.
-   Semi-final winners advance to the final.
-   Semi-final losers contest the third-place playoff.
-   The final winner is champion; the final loser is runner-up.
-   The third-place playoff winner receives third place.

## 5. Match duration and tie resolution

-   Regulation time: 20 minutes first half and 20 minutes second half.
-   A tied knockout match proceeds to five minutes extra time.
-   If still tied, a penalty shootout decides the winner.
-   The detailed penalty sequence and spot distance must be published
    before the first match.

## 6. Walkover

A team has a 15-minute grace period after scheduled kickoff. After that
period, the referee/match coordinator records non-appearance and refers
the matter to the Committee. The walkover score and any additional
consequences are **\[TO CONFIRM\]** and must be published before the
tournament.

## 7. Result confirmation

The referee records the result. The Match Coordinator checks and
publishes it. Any formal protest must follow the Disciplinary and
Appeals Policy. Public results should include: - Round and match
number. - Team names. - Full-time score. - Extra-time score, if
applicable. - Penalty score, if applicable. - Match date and time. -
Cards and notable disciplinary events where appropriate.

## 8. Suggested schedule framework

The competition must run for at least two weeks. The final calendar may
span 2--4 weeks. A possible sequence: - Week 1: Round of 16. - Week 2:
Quarter-finals. - Week 3: Semi-finals. - Week 3 or 4: Third-place
playoff and final.

This is a planning framework, not a confirmed calendar. Schedule matches
around venue availability and academic commitments, and allow reasonable
recovery time.

## 9. Public bracket

The public platform or social pages may show team names, bracket
positions, fixtures, scores and progression. Do not publish private
player contact details or student identity documents.
