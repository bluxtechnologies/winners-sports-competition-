# 09 --- Video Review and Media Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

Phone recording supports match documentation, limited incident review
and volunteer media coverage. It is not a professional VAR system and
may not capture every incident or angle.

## 2. Review arrangement

-   Matches may be recorded using a designated phone.
-   A camera operator should be assigned where available.
-   The phone should be positioned safely to capture as much of the
    playing area as possible.
-   The referee may initiate a review.
-   Each team may make one review appeal request per match.
-   The detailed review categories and process must be approved before
    the first match.

## 3. Proposed review categories

The organizer may approve review for: - Whether a goal was scored. -
Serious foul play or violent conduct. - Possible red-card incidents. -
Mistaken player identity. - Other clearly defined major incidents.

Whether ordinary fouls, penalty decisions or boundary decisions are
reviewable must be explicitly settled before the tournament.

## 4. Review procedure

Proposed procedure: 1. Captain makes the team's one appeal request to
the referee. 2. The referee decides whether the incident falls within
the review scope. 3. Play is paused only where the referee considers it
appropriate. 4. The designated operator retrieves the relevant footage.
5. The referee views the available footage. 6. The referee communicates
the decision. 7. The review request and outcome are recorded.

The organizer must decide whether an appeal can be made only before play
restarts, and whether a review can be requested after the final whistle.
The final authority must be stated clearly in the approved rules.

## 5. Limitations

-   Footage may be obstructed, unclear or incomplete.
-   There may be no alternate camera angle.
-   The phone recording does not guarantee that every disputed incident
    can be resolved.
-   A review should not be used to delay play or pressure officials.
-   The competition should not promise professional VAR accuracy.

## 6. Media platforms and volunteers

Official platforms: Instagram and TikTok. Friends will volunteer to
support: - Pre-match posters and announcements. - Team introductions. -
Matchday photos and short clips. - Goals, saves and key moments. -
Post-match results and highlights. - Short player/captain interviews. -
Tournament updates and final coverage.

A Media Coordinator should agree who is covering each match, who can
publish, and how content is handed over.

## 7. Consent and privacy

Before recording and publishing: - Give participants clear notice that
matches may be filmed and photographed. - Obtain any consent required by
applicable institutional rules or law. - Avoid publishing sensitive
personal information. - Do not post private registration records, phone
numbers, emails, matric numbers or payment evidence. - Establish a
contact route for media/privacy concerns. - Set a reasonable footage
retention period and restrict access to review footage.

## 8. Brand consistency

Use the official tournament name, logo and approved colors once
finalized. Keep score graphics, fixtures and highlights readable and
consistent. Do not imply that a sponsor or institution has endorsed the
competition unless permission has been obtained.
