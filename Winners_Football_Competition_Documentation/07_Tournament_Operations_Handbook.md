# 07 --- Tournament Operations Handbook

## 1. Operating principle

The first edition should be lean, safe, transparent and reliable. The
tournament is student-focused. Volunteer friends will support media
coverage, and no trophy or medals are planned.

## 2. Suggested organizing roles

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Role                                Main responsibility
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Tournament Director                 Overall coordination and decisions

  Registration Officer                Team/player records and eligibility

  Finance Officer                     Bank verification, receipts, budget
                                      and prize records

  Match Coordinator                   Fixtures, kickoff readiness and
                                      results

  Referee Coordinator                 Referee appointments and briefings

  Disciplinary Panel                  Protests, misconduct and sanctions

  Media Coordinator                   Volunteer schedule, recording and
                                      social posts

  First-aid contact                   Initial response and emergency
                                      escalation
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Roles may be combined where the team is small, but keep payment
verification and financial reporting transparent.

## 3. Before registration opens

-   Confirm venue permission and available dates.
-   Inspect the playing area and goalposts.
-   Confirm match rules and unresolved items in the Rules of the Game.
-   Confirm the referee and match-official arrangements.
-   Confirm first-aid and emergency transport arrangements.
-   Approve bank details and payment-verification process.
-   Publish registration deadline and refund policy.
-   Publish Terms and Conditions and media notice.
-   Assign the volunteer media coordinator.

## 4. Before the tournament draw

-   Confirm 16 eligible teams.
-   Confirm seven players per team.
-   Verify all full registration payments.
-   Resolve duplicate player records.
-   Freeze the accepted roster list.
-   Publish the draw date, method and witnesses.
-   Conduct a random draw and record the result.

## 5. Matchday checklist

### Venue and safety

-   Playing surface checked.
-   Goalposts stable and safe.
-   Boundaries visible.
-   Ball checked.
-   First-aid kit and responder available.
-   Emergency contact/transport plan available.
-   Water and basic participant welfare arrangements considered.

### Administration

-   Fixture and team names confirmed.
-   Referee present and briefed.
-   Team rosters available to officials.
-   Captains present.
-   Match record sheet ready.
-   Phone recording device charged and storage available.
-   Media volunteers briefed.

### Before kickoff

-   Confirm five players from each team.
-   Confirm substitutes are registered.
-   Check that suspended players do not participate.
-   Explain substitution limit and permanent-substitution rule.
-   Remind teams of match time and review procedure.

## 6. During the match

The referee records: - Starting teams. - Goals and approximate times. -
Substitutions. - Yellow and red cards. - Injuries and stoppages. - Video
review requests and outcomes. - Abandonment or unusual incidents.

## 7. After the match

-   Referee confirms the result with both captains where practical.
-   Match Coordinator enters the result.
-   Finance Officer records any fines.
-   Disciplinary Panel receives incident reports.
-   Media Coordinator publishes approved score/highlights.
-   Fixtures are updated for the next round.

## 8. Scheduling

The competition must last at least two weeks. A 2--4 week planning
window is acceptable, depending on venue availability, student schedules
and match capacity. Do not publish a final calendar until the venue and
officials are confirmed.

## 9. Volunteer management

Volunteer friends should receive a clear schedule, role assignment and
contact person. Agree in advance on transport/data/refreshment support,
if any. Do not assume volunteer availability without confirmation.

## 10. Incident management

For serious injury, violence, unsafe conditions, weather or equipment
failure: 1. Stop play if necessary. 2. Protect participants and
spectators. 3. Contact the designated first-aid/emergency support. 4.
Inform the Tournament Director. 5. Record the incident and witnesses. 6.
Resume only when the referee and organizer consider it safe. 7. Apply
the match-abandonment policy.

## 11. End-of-tournament closeout

-   Confirm final standings and results.
-   Verify prize recipients.
-   Pay prizes using the published method and deadline.
-   Reconcile registration income and expenses.
-   Close outstanding disciplinary matters.
-   Archive approved match records and media.
-   Collect feedback for the next edition.
