# 10 --- Financial Management and Prize Policy

## 1. Revenue

Registration fee: ₦10,000 per team.

At full capacity: 16 teams × ₦10,000 = **₦160,000 gross registration
income**.

This projection assumes all 16 teams pay in full and their payments are
verified.

## 2. Prize commitments

  Position              Prize
  ------------- -------------
  Champion            ₦50,000
  Runner-up           ₦20,000
  Third place         ₦10,000
  **Total**       **₦80,000**

The prize pool represents 50% of the projected registration income. The
remaining ₦80,000 is the maximum amount available for operating costs
before any sponsorship or other income.

## 3. Illustrative operating budget

The following is a planning example, not a quote or approved spend.

  Expense                                 Planning allowance
  ----------------------------- ----------------------------
  Referee and match officials                        ₦15,000
  Venue/pitch arrangements                           ₦15,000
  First aid and safety                                ₦7,000
  Media support (if needed)                       ₦0--₦8,000
  Contingency                     Balance of operating funds
  **Operating ceiling**                          **₦80,000**

No trophy or medals are planned for the first edition. Media is
supported by friends volunteering; any transport, data or refreshment
support should be agreed in advance and budgeted only if affordable.

## 4. Payment verification

1.  The organizer publishes approved bank details.
2.  Teams transfer the full ₦10,000 fee.
3.  Teams submit payment reference/evidence.
4.  Finance Officer verifies actual receipt in the account.
5.  Registration is confirmed only after verification.
6.  A receipt or confirmation message is issued.
7.  Payment records are kept securely.

A screenshot alone is not proof of cleared funds.

## 5. Financial controls

-   Keep tournament funds separate from personal spending where
    practical.
-   Record every receipt and expense.
-   Keep evidence for payments and purchases.
-   Require a second organizer to review the financial summary.
-   Do not treat card fines as guaranteed income.
-   Do not spend prize money on operations.
-   Do not announce sponsorship income until it is confirmed.

## 6. Card fines

-   Yellow card: ₦1,000.
-   Red card: ₦3,000. Fines are recorded and collected by the designated
    Finance Officer through the official process. The referee does not
    collect cash on the pitch. The Committee must publish any
    consequences for unpaid fines before the competition begins.

## 7. Prize payment

The organizer must publish: - Who is entitled to receive each prize. -
Whether payment is made to the captain or another nominated recipient. -
Required confirmation/receipt. - Payment method. - Payment deadline
after the final and any outstanding protest.

Recommended practice: verify the recipient with the captain and obtain
an acknowledgment when prize money is paid.

## 8. Refunds and cancellations

A written refund policy must be approved before registration opens. It
must cover: - Organizer cancellation. - Venue unavailability or
prolonged postponement. - Team withdrawal. - Rejected or ineligible
registration. - Duplicate or mistaken payment. - Failure to fill all 16
slots.

Do not advertise a non-refundable fee until the policy has been reviewed
and clearly communicated.

## 9. Closeout report

After the tournament, prepare a simple report showing: - Number of teams
paid and confirmed. - Total registration income. - Sponsorship or other
income, if any. - Prize payments. - Operating expenses. - Outstanding
fines, if tracked. - Final balance. - Any unpaid commitments.
