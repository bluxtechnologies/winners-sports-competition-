# 02 --- Tournament Constitution

## Article 1 --- Name and status

1.  The competition shall be known as the **Winners Football Competition
    (WFC)**.
2.  The first edition shall be hosted at Southern Delta University,
    Ozoro.
3.  WFC is a student competition open only to eligible current students
    of the institution.

## Article 2 --- Objectives

WFC exists to provide a fair, organized and enjoyable campus football
competition, encourage sportsmanship, and create a foundation for future
editions.

## Article 3 --- Governing documents

The competition shall be governed by: 1. This Constitution. 2. The Rules
of the Game. 3. The Terms and Conditions accepted during registration.
4. The Disciplinary and Appeals Policy. 5. Official notices issued by
the Tournament Committee.

If documents conflict, the Tournament Committee must clarify the rule in
writing before the affected match. No clarification may retrospectively
change a completed match result.

## Article 4 --- Tournament Committee

The organizer shall appoint a Tournament Committee. Recommended roles: -
Tournament Director: overall coordination and final administrative
oversight. - Registration/Finance Officer: registrations, payment
verification and financial records. - Fixtures/Match Coordinator: draw,
schedule and results. - Referee Coordinator: referee appointment and
communication. - Disciplinary/Protest Panel: handles formal complaints
and sanctions. - Media Coordinator: coordinates volunteer media
coverage.

One person may hold more than one administrative role if necessary, but
financial records and disciplinary decisions should have oversight by at
least one other committee member.

## Article 5 --- Team entry

1.  Sixteen teams are required for the planned competition.
2.  Each team shall register seven players.
3.  Each team shall nominate one captain as its official contact.
4.  A team is confirmed only after its registration has been reviewed
    and its ₦10,000 fee has been received and verified.
5.  The draw shall be random among all 16 accepted teams.
6.  No team may use an unregistered player.

## Article 6 --- Player eligibility

1.  Only current students of Southern Delta University, Ozoro may
    participate.
2.  The organizer shall publish the accepted student-verification method
    before registration opens.
3.  Each player may represent only one team in this edition.
4.  A player may not be registered on multiple team rosters.
5.  The roster deadline and any exceptional replacement process must be
    published before registration opens.

## Article 7 --- Match administration

1.  Matches shall be played under the Rules of the Game.
2.  Each team shall have five players participating at a time.
3.  There is no fixed goalkeeper or fixed post defender; all five
    players may move freely.
4.  Each team may make no more than two permanent substitutions per
    match.
5.  Regulation time is 20 minutes per half.
6.  A tied knockout match proceeds to five minutes of extra time and
    then a penalty shootout if still tied.
7.  The referee controls the match and applies the published rules.

## Article 8 --- Walkovers and withdrawals

1.  A team has a 15-minute grace period from the scheduled kickoff time
    to report ready to play.
2.  If the team has not appeared after the grace period, the referee or
    match coordinator shall record the facts and the Committee may award
    a walkover.
3.  The walkover score and consequences of repeated non-appearance must
    be published before the tournament begins.
4.  Withdrawal, cancellation and refund handling shall follow the
    published Terms and Conditions.

## Article 9 --- Discipline and disputes

1.  Yellow-card fine: ₦1,000.
2.  Red-card fine: ₦3,000 and automatic suspension from the next match.
3.  Fines shall be administered by the Finance Officer, not collected
    personally by the referee.
4.  Sporting suspensions are separate from financial fines.
5.  Protests and appeals shall follow the Disciplinary and Appeals
    Policy.
6.  Serious misconduct may result in further sanctions after a fair
    review.

## Article 10 --- Video review and media

1.  Matches may be recorded using a phone for the competition's
    video-review and media purposes.
2.  The referee may initiate a review.
3.  Each team may make one review appeal request per match.
4.  Review scope, procedure and final authority shall be published in
    the Video Review Guidelines before kickoff.
5.  Media publication shall follow the consent and privacy notice.

## Article 11 --- Prizes

The announced prizes are ₦50,000 for the champion, ₦20,000 for the
runner-up and ₦10,000 for third place. No trophy or medals are planned
for the first edition. Prize payment timing and method shall be
announced before the final.

## Article 12 --- Amendments

The Committee may issue clarifications and amend future operating
procedures. Material rule changes must be communicated to all captains
before they take effect. Changes must not be applied retrospectively to
change completed match outcomes.

## Article 13 --- Acceptance

By registering and participating, each team and player agrees to comply
with the approved Constitution, Rules of the Game, Terms and Conditions
and official competition notices.
