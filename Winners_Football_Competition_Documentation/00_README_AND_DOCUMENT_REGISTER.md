## First Edition --- Documentation Package

**Host institution:** Southern Delta University, Ozoro\
**Competition format:** 5-a-side Monkey Post football,
single-elimination knockout\
**Document status:** Working draft for organizer review\
**Version:** 0.1

## Purpose

This package provides the working documentation for planning and
operating the first edition of the Winners Football Competition (WFC).
It is based on decisions supplied by the organizer. Items marked **\[TO
CONFIRM\]** are not settled and must be approved before registration
opens or before the relevant match.

## Locked decisions

-   Name: **Winners Football Competition** (WFC).
-   Host: Southern Delta University, Ozoro.
-   Eligibility: current students of the institution only.
-   16 teams; 7 registered players per team.
-   5 players play at a time; all five may move freely. There is no
    fixed goalkeeper or fixed post defender.
-   Monkey Post format: small goalposts; players do not use their hands
    to keep/stop the ball.
-   2 substitutes; maximum 2 substitutions per match; substitutions are
    permanent. A substituted player cannot return in that match.
-   Regulation match time: 20 minutes first half + 20 minutes second
    half.
-   Half-time duration: **\[TO CONFIRM\]**.
-   A tied knockout match: 5 minutes extra time, then penalty kicks if
    still tied.
-   Penalty kicks: direct kicks from a designated long-distance penalty
    spot toward the small goal.
-   Registration fee: ₦10,000 per team; full payment before registration
    is confirmed; bank transfer with admin verification.
-   Random draw involving all 16 accepted teams.
-   15-minute grace period for a team to appear; walkover follows after
    the grace period, subject to the match official confirming
    non-appearance.
-   Yellow card fine: ₦1,000.
-   Red card fine: ₦3,000 and automatic suspension from the next match.
-   Video review: phone recording; referee may initiate review; each
    team has one appeal request per match.
-   Duration: at least 2 weeks; planning window may be 2--4 weeks
    depending on venue and academic schedules.
-   Separate third-place playoff.
-   Media coverage is handled by friends volunteering; platforms are
    Instagram and TikTok.
-   No trophy or medals for the first edition.

## Important items not yet approved

Do not present these as settled rules until the organizer confirms
them: - Exact competition dates, venue and pitch dimensions. - Goal
dimensions and exact penalty-spot distance. - Ball size/type. -
Half-time duration and interval between matches. - Whether extra time is
one continuous 5-minute period or divided. - Exact penalty shootout
sequence and eligible kickers. - What constitutes a goal, including
whether any height/under-bar restriction applies. - Ball-out, restart,
kick-in, foul, handball and dangerous-play rules. - Whether a player
dismissed with a red card may be replaced, and when. - Yellow-card
accumulation across different matches. - Protest deadline and who
decides appeals. - Detailed video-review categories and whether the
referee's decision is final. - Walkover score and consequences for
repeated no-shows. - Refund/cancellation policy. - Student identity
verification method. - Privacy, media consent and footage retention
arrangements. - First-aid provider and emergency transport plan.

## Document control

The Tournament Director should maintain one approved master copy.
Changes must be dated, versioned and communicated to all team captains
before they take effect. No rule should be changed retrospectively to
alter the outcome of a completed match.
