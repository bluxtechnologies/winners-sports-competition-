# 06 --- Disciplinary and Appeals Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how WFC will record misconduct, administer card
fines, impose suspensions and handle formal complaints.

## 2. Card fines

  Card              Fine
  ------------- --------
  Yellow card     ₦1,000
  Red card        ₦3,000

Fines are paid to the official tournament account or Finance Officer
using the published process. Referees must not personally collect fines.
A fine does not cancel, replace or reduce a sporting suspension.

## 3. Red cards

1.  A player shown a red card is dismissed from the match.
2.  The player receives a ₦3,000 fine.
3.  The player is automatically suspended from the team's next match.
4.  The Committee may impose further sanctions for serious misconduct
    after reviewing the incident and giving the affected person an
    opportunity to respond.
5.  Whether a dismissed player may be replaced during the match is
    **\[TO CONFIRM\]** in the Rules of the Game.

## 4. Yellow cards

1.  Each yellow card carries a ₦1,000 fine.
2.  The treatment of a second yellow in the same match is **\[TO
    CONFIRM\]**.
3.  Accumulation of yellow cards across different matches and any
    resulting suspension are **\[TO CONFIRM\]**.
4.  The referee must record the player's identity, team, match and time
    of the card.

## 5. Serious misconduct

Examples include: - Physical assault or attempted assault. - Threats or
intimidation. - Deliberate dangerous play. - Abuse or harassment of
officials or participants. - Fielding an ineligible or unregistered
player. - Match-fixing, bribery or deliberate manipulation. - Refusal to
comply with a lawful match official instruction.

Possible outcomes include warning, fine, match suspension,
multiple-match suspension, team sanction or expulsion. The Committee
must consider the seriousness and available evidence and record its
decision.

## 6. Team protests and appeals

Proposed procedure, subject to organizer approval: 1. Only the team
captain may submit a formal protest on behalf of the team. 2. A protest
must identify the match, incident, rule relied upon and requested
remedy. 3. Proposed deadline: within 30 minutes after the match ends
**\[TO CONFIRM\]**. 4. A protest should not be used simply to challenge
an ordinary judgment call by the referee. 5. The Disciplinary/Protest
Panel should review the written submission, referee report and available
footage. 6. The decision and reasons should be recorded and communicated
to both teams. 7. The Committee should publish whether a further appeal
is permitted and its deadline.

## 7. Video review requests

Each team has one review appeal request per match. The referee may also
initiate a review. The detailed scope and process are governed by the
Video Review and Media Guidelines.

## 8. Fair process

A person or team facing a serious sanction should be told the allegation
and given a reasonable opportunity to respond. Decisions should be based
on available evidence, applied consistently and recorded.

## 9. Fine collection and records

The Finance Officer shall maintain a register of fines assessed,
payments received, outstanding amounts and receipts. Any consequence for
unpaid fines must be published before the tournament begins.
