# 04 --- Team and Player Terms & Conditions

**Competition:** Winners Football Competition (WFC)\
**Host:** Southern Delta University, Ozoro

By submitting a team registration or participating in a WFC match, the
team captain and registered players agree to the following terms.

## 1. Eligibility

-   Only current students of Southern Delta University, Ozoro may
    participate.
-   Each player must provide the required student-verification
    information.
-   The organizer may reject or remove a registration that does not
    satisfy eligibility requirements.

## 2. Team roster

-   Each team must register seven players.
-   Five players participate at a time.
-   A player may represent only one team in the competition.
-   Teams may not field unregistered or ineligible players.
-   Roster changes after the published deadline are not permitted except
    under a written exception approved by the Committee.

## 3. Registration and payment

-   The team registration fee is ₦10,000.
-   Full payment is required before registration is confirmed.
-   Payment is made by bank transfer using the official payment
    instructions supplied by the organizer.
-   Teams must submit a valid payment reference or evidence for
    administrative verification.
-   A transfer screenshot alone does not constitute confirmation until
    the Finance Officer verifies receipt.

## 4. Rules and conduct

-   Teams and players must comply with the Constitution, Rules of the
    Game and official notices.
-   Players must respect referees, opponents, volunteers, spectators and
    organizers.
-   Fighting, threats, harassment, discrimination, deliberate damage and
    dangerous conduct are prohibited.
-   Players must comply with card sanctions, fines and suspensions.

## 5. Attendance and walkovers

-   Teams must be ready at the scheduled venue and time.
-   A 15-minute grace period applies.
-   Failure to appear after the grace period may result in a walkover.
-   The walkover score and consequences must be communicated in the
    final competition rules.

## 6. Match decisions and complaints

-   The referee manages on-field decisions.
-   Video review is limited to the published review process and
    available footage.
-   Formal protests must follow the Disciplinary and Appeals Policy.
-   Abuse or intimidation of officials is prohibited.

## 7. Safety and injury

-   Participants must take reasonable care of themselves and others.
-   Players should disclose relevant immediate safety concerns to the
    organizer before playing.
-   The organizer will publish the available first-aid and emergency
    arrangements.
-   Players must stop playing when instructed by the referee for a
    safety reason.

## 8. Photography, video and publication

-   Matches may be recorded for competition review, highlights and event
    reporting.
-   The organizer must provide a clear notice explaining intended use of
    images and footage.
-   Participants should be given a contact route for questions or
    concerns about media use.
-   Any separate consent required by applicable institutional rules or
    law must be obtained.

## 9. Refunds and cancellation

The refund policy is **\[TO CONFIRM\]** and must be published before
payment is collected. The organizer must state what happens if the event
is cancelled, postponed, a team withdraws or a team is rejected after
payment.

## 10. Prizes

The announced prizes are: - Champion: ₦50,000. - Runner-up: ₦20,000. -
Third place: ₦10,000.

No trophy or medals are planned for the first edition. Prize payment
method and deadline will be announced by the organizer.

## 11. Acceptance

The captain confirms that the team has informed all registered players
of these terms and the competition rules. Registration is not complete
until payment and eligibility checks are approved.
